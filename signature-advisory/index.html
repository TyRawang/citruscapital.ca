Questions

Common questions.

Is this the same as your accounting or advisory services?
No. Signature Advisory is a separate, premium engagement. If you need bookkeeping or accounting, that is handled through our Accounting and Bookkeeping service. If you need strategic support without the full Signature commitment, our Advisory service may be the right starting point.
What industries do you work with?
Virtually all of them. Lisa has advised businesses across retail, hospitality, professional services, real estate, e-commerce, and beyond. Strategic and financial advisory is largely industry-agnostic at its core.
How long does an engagement last?
Engagements require a minimum six-month commitment. This is not a project or a trial — it is a relationship, and relationships take time to deliver compounding value. Most clients continue well beyond the initial term.
What does it cost?
Signature Advisory starts at $5,000 per month. The exact structure is discussed directly and built around the scope of your engagement.
What if I am not accepted?
If Signature Advisory is not the right fit, we will say so directly. In many cases, our Advisory service is the right starting point, and we will tell you that.
