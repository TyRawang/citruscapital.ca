Accounting & Bookkeeping

So you can run the business, not reconcile it.

Whether you’re running a boutique, a restaurant, a construction company, or a professional services firm: your books need to be clean, current, and readable. We’ve worked with businesses across all industries and all sizes. Our clients don’t manage their own books, because they know their time is better spent running their business. They make data-informed decisions based on up-to-date, timely financial information.

Bookkeeping Services

The operational foundation of your finances: keeping your records accurate, current, and organized.

  • Transaction categorization and reconciliation
  • Payroll processing and reporting
  • Accounts payable and receivable management
  • Month-end and year-end close

Accounting Services

The reporting layer: preparing and presenting your financial picture accurately and completely.

  • Financial statement preparation (income statement, balance sheet, cash flow)
  • Audit preparation
  • Year-end working paper preparation
  • Chart of accounts setup and maintenance
  • Fixed asset tracking and depreciation schedules
  • GST/HST filing and compliance
  • Management reporting packages
  • Bank reconciliations

There are two kinds of business owners.

Those whose books are always a few months behind: scrambled together when someone asks for them. And those whose numbers are always current, always clean, and ready to inform the next decision. The difference isn’t the size of the business. It’s whether someone owns the books or not.

Personal service, every time.

At Citrus, every client has a direct, ongoing relationship with their account manager: someone who knows your business, knows your numbers, and is reachable when you need them. That’s not a feature. It’s how we work.

Limited availability each quarter.
