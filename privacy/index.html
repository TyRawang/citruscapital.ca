Privacy Notice

Citrus Capital Partners (“Citrus Capital”, “we”) respects your privacy. This notice explains what information this website collects and how it is used. It applies to citruscapital.ca only.

What we collect

Information you send us. When you use the contact form or the Signature Advisory application, we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address, business name and the message or answers you provide. This information is stored in HubSpot, our customer relationship management system, and is used only to respond to you and to manage any engagement that follows.

Analytics. We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed and for how long. IP addresses are anonymized. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

Cookies. Analytics and HubSpot may set cookies in your browser. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will continue to work.

Third parties

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The contact map is provided by Google Maps and fonts by Google Fonts, each of which may receive your IP address when the page loads. We do not sell or share your information with anyone else.

Retention and access

We keep form submissions for as long as needed to respond to you and to meet our legal and professional obligations. You may ask us at any time what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing hello@citruscapital.ca.
