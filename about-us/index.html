About Us

Our Story

Citrus Capital was inspired by the luscious growth of the citrus trees in Southern California. This reflects exactly who we are – passionate people, with fresh ideas, delivered with excitement and clarity.

Since its inception in 2012, Citrus has offered a wide variety of support to a wide variety of businesses. Our experience includes work in retail, specialty manufacturing, technology, real estate, construction services, corporate finance, master planning and economic development, energy and private equity. We have guided businesses from start up to shareholder successions, acquisitions to new market expansion and private equity investment.
We are entrepreneurs, working with other entrepreneurs, and we get it. We’ve been there too.

Whether you’re stuck in the grind, wearing too many hats, overworked or looking to others for answers. We learned that the power of collaboration and alignment leads to exceptional business outcomes and we are here to help.

Our Experience

We have experience managing, growing, and financing small to medium-sized businesses, making Citrus Capital the go-to resource for Calgary entrepreneurs looking to take their business to the next level. Our experience includes work in corporate finance, private equity, energy, technology, retail, CPG, real estate, specialty manufacturing, marketing, master planning and economic development. We have guided businesses through acquisitions and divestitures, shareholder successions, private equity investment, and more.

Today that experience shows up in three ways: accounting and bookkeeping that keeps your numbers current, advisory that turns them into decisions, and coaching for the owner behind the business. For a deeper, ongoing partnership there is Signature Advisory.
